Privacy at Apex Trio

This page explains what happens to the details you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you send the project form we receive your name, email, the phone or WhatsApp number and business name if you give them, the service you picked and your message. When you subscribe to the newsletter we receive your email address.

What we use it for

We use these details only to answer your inquiry, to prepare a quote, and to send the newsletter you asked for. We do not sell or share them with advertisers.

Where it is kept

Form messages are delivered to our team inbox by email. The website itself stores no form data and sets no tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time with the link in each email, or ask us to delete your details by replying to any message from us.
