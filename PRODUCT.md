# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Multi-page static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed on Cloudflare Pages from GitHub. One Cloudflare Pages Function (`functions/api/contact.js`) sends contact-form inquiries by email through Resend. Existing Base44 URLs (/services, /offerings, /about, /work, /testimonials, /contact, /services/<slug>) are kept.

## Users

Owners and managers of local businesses in Panglao and the wider island of Bohol, Philippines: resorts, dive shops, cafes, restaurants, tour operators, shops. They are busy running the business, often on a phone, and want more walk-ins, bookings and orders from people who search on Facebook, Instagram, Google and AI assistants. They choose a partner they can meet and trust.

## Product Purpose

Apex Trio Digital Partners is a social media, web and AI-search studio from Panglao, Bohol. It helps local businesses strengthen their digital presence, communicate their value, and become easier for customers to find and choose. Success: a prospect sends an inquiry through the contact form.

## Positioning

A three-person local studio based in Panglao itself ("Panglao first, then scale"): strategy, execution and technology in the same room, starting with the smallest system that makes a measurable difference rather than bloated retainers. Explicitly covers AI-powered search visibility alongside Google and social.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four service lines:
1. Social Media Marketing: monthly content calendar, 12-20 posts and reels, community management, monthly performance report.
2. Website Design & Development: mobile-first sites, built to convert, client controls content.
3. AI Search & Digital Visibility: local search audit, listing and profile cleanup, structured content, visibility reporting.
4. Automation & Integrations (new as a service line): business automation and workflow integration, social media automation, automated posting and content management, CRM management and automation, lead generation and inquiry automation, email and communication automation, business process automation, AI-powered business automation, website and application integration.

Offerings: Visibility Starter (package: one-page website, business profile cleanup, 30-day content plan) and Growth Partnership (retainer: monthly content and campaigns, website upkeep, search and AI visibility tracking). Pricing: contact for pricing. Replies usually within one business day. An inquiry is not a payment.
